Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Jarvis Content OS is a personal AI-assisted content workflow. This policy explains how information is handled when a user connects supported social platforms.

Information processed

  • Account authorization data required by supported platform APIs, such as OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Basic account information returned by authorized APIs when needed for the requested feature.
  • Videos, captions, metadata and publishing settings that the user chooses to process.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested content preparation, account connection, draft upload and publishing-related functionality. Jarvis Content OS does not sell personal data.

Storage and security

Credentials and platform tokens are kept outside this public website and are not committed to this repository. Public pages contain no account secrets. Access is limited to the application environment used by the account owner.

Third-party services

When the user connects TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or another supported service, that service's own privacy policy and terms also apply.

User control

Users can revoke platform authorization through the relevant platform account settings. Stored authorization data can then be removed from the Jarvis Content OS environment.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the public project repository at github.com/Lukashydra/jarvis-content-os.
