2. What Jarvis Content OS is

Jarvis Content OS is a pre-release AI-assisted creator workflow for preparing, reviewing and sending short-form video content to supported social platforms through official APIs.

3. Categories of data processed

  • OAuth authorization data such as access and refresh tokens when a creator connects a supported platform.
  • Basic platform account information returned by authorized APIs where required for the requested integration.
  • Videos, audio, transcripts, captions, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing settings supplied or generated for the creator workflow.
  • Technical request data generated when this public website is accessed.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Requested integration and workflow functions: processing required to provide creator-requested features and platform connections. Where a contractual relationship exists or is being prepared, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Security and technical operation: limited technical processing may be based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for secure, reliable operation and abuse prevention.
  • Consent-based platform access: OAuth authorization is initiated by the creator and can be revoked through the relevant platform. Where consent is the appropriate legal basis, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR applies.
